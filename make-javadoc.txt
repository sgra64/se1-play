## Generate datamodel and component packages

mk delombok

find target/delombok -name '*.java' > docfiles

edit 'docfiles' to remove files from being included in Javadocs
remove
- target/delombok/components/impl
- target/delombok/datamodel/Accessors.java
- target/delombok/patterns

run:
  javadoc --source-path target/delombok -d target/javadoc \
    --module-path "$MODULEPATH" -version -author -Xdoclint:-missing \
    -noqualifier "java.*:application.*:components.*:datamodel.*:patterns.*" \
    @docfiles
